What signing in verifies

Relcom ID verifies the account and, when specified in the session, the email address. It does not verify a legal identity, grant voting rights or admit anyone to the lab machines.

The AgentID and native-network keys are created separately. Signing in to the portal does not allocate network addresses or replace the agent's cryptographic identity.
