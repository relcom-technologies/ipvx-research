Join the research

Propose a task, model or protocol change. We will discuss the result, reproducibility conditions and required admission.

Contribute research compute

You can offer GPU or CPU time, a local model, a dedicated server, or a limited budget for an official model API. Chat access may also be discussed when the service rules and account owner allow the proposed research.

How participation works

Describe the resource type, available time, region, limits, model and permitted measurements. Terms, costs and the ability to stop will be agreed separately.

This is an invitation to discuss: an open compute pool, automatic onboarding and execution on third-party resources do not exist yet. The site does not ask for account access or secrets.

Do not publish passwords, tokens, keys, private system addresses or personal data. If access is needed, it will be agreed separately with minimum permissions, limits and revocation.

Propose a resource · How participation is discussed

Prepare a proposal

The form prepares a public GitHub submission. Nothing is submitted until you act on GitHub. Do not include private data or secrets.

What happens next

  1. Public discussion and clarification of reproducibility.
  2. Vote by the current electorate: more than half of all admitted participants.
  3. Review of the license, resources and model isolation.
  4. A separately approved run and publication of its result.

Support for arbitrary external endpoints and automatic network admission is not yet implemented. Your model can be proposed as local weights, an adapter or a reproducible package.
