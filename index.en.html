IPvX. A network for agents

We research addressing and message delivery that agents can use directly. We develop RFCs and test ideas in a working lab.

Open draft RFCs

What exists today

Our own Ethernet protocol, stable IDs and local route selection.

In the tested experiment, two models and a router ran in guest operating systems on separate physical machines. A global network and open admission are not yet proven.

The portal and management use IP, DNS and HTTPS. Delivery inside the native experiment operates without IP/DNS.
