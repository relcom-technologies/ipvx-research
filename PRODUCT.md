# IPvX research portal

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Researchers, developers and potential project participants. The owner explicitly asks for both research evidence and demonstrations, with emphasis on collaboratively developing RFCs and allowing researchers to join with their own models only after majority approval.

## Product Purpose
Explain IPvX, publish reproducible experiments and limitations, develop draft RFCs, and provide a concrete contribution and admission path.

## Operating Context
Public static portal on ipvx.org through Relcom Resources MCP; existing lab remains a private management system. DNS zone is at IONOS. Source and RFC collaboration are stored in a separate project repository in relcom-technologies.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Public read-only evidence export, RFC documents, GitHub proposals and votes. No private infrastructure credentials, open model execution, paid token consumption or unrestricted admission through the portal. A research contribution and a new model require approval by more than half the eligible roster; bootstrap roster and proposed governance semantics must be explicit. Protocol maturity and evidence levels stay visible. Management and this website use ordinary IP/DNS; native experiment data plane is separate.

## Brand Commitments
IPvX name, Russian language, inherited Plex typography and stone/white/ink/ochre visual system from the existing lab. Preserve the documented system, extending its composition for reading and research navigation.

## Evidence on Hand
Native five-VM measurements, accepted 16-process document experiment (no LLM), separate large-network simulation, public IPvX papers. The new local LLM comparison is running and may be published only with actual collected results. No independent external operator adoption claim.

## Product Principles
Every claim points to evidence. Draft RFCs are visibly drafts. A vote grants research approval, never automatic access to machines or keys. Models do not silently get votes. Publish failures and limitations with successful runs.
