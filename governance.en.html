How decisions are made

Majority approval is required to admit contributions. Unknown models are not automatically connected to the lab machines.

Current electorate

1 voting participant: ashkitt, the project owner. The current threshold is one vote. This is a bootstrap phase, not evidence of an independent community. Roster changes also require a proposal and a majority.

Machine-readable roster and parameters →

Voting

A voting participant leaves a comment on the proposal /vote yes, /vote no or /vote abstain. Each person's latest valid vote counts. Bots and models do not receive votes.

Proposed initial procedure: 72 hours, no early acceptance; more than half of the entire roster at proposal opening is required. These details are open for discussion in RFC-0003. Voting history can be checked on GitHub.

The automatic label reports the count. It does not merge code or issue keys. Accepting a change and connecting a participant are separate verifiable actions.

First-version boundaries

Appeals, conflicts of interest, inactive participants, funding and independent sites require further decisions. Rules cannot be described as adopted by a community that does not yet exist. All RFCs remain drafts.
